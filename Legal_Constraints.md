Legal Constraints

All three of our datasets were produced by the Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO) and are distributed through the World Bank’s Data360 platform. FAO holds the copyright, and we found the license for each dataset in its Data360 metadata. The two price datasets, food price inflation (FAO_CP_23014) and consumer prices, food indices (FAO_CP_23013), are licensed under CC BY 4.0. The food insecurity dataset (FAO_FS_210081) has a stricter license, CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO.

The CC BY 4.0 license allows us to copy, adapt, and share the price data as long as we credit FAO and note any changes we make. The food insecurity license also requires us to credit FAO, but it adds two more conditions. We can only use the data for non-commercial purposes, and any adapted version we share has to be released under the same license. All three datasets are also covered by the FAO Statistical Database Terms of Use and the World Bank Terms of Use for Datasets. Under these terms, we cannot imply that FAO or the World Bank endorses our work, we cannot misrepresent the data, and the data is provided without any warranty.

Since our project is non-commercial academic work, we are allowed to use all three datasets. However, because our merged dataset includes the food insecurity data, we will have to release it under CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO. In our README, we will cite all three datasets, describe the changes we made to the data, and state that our analysis is not endorsed by FAO or the World Bank. Our code will be licensed separately from the data. Finally, the datasets only contain country-level totals and no information about individuals, so there are no privacy concerns.

We will cite each dataset in the format FAO requires. For example: FAO. 2026. Suite of Food Security Indicators: Moderately or severely food insecure people (million) (3-year average). Accessed on 17 November 2026. https://data360.worldbank.org/en/indicator/FAO_FS_210081. Licence: CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO.
