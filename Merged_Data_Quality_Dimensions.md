<h2>Completeness</h2>
The merged dataset has no missing values for “REF_AREA,” which identifies the country, and no missing values for the year described. However, missing values are present in all three of our analytical variables. The 3-year consumer price variable has 431 missing observations, the 3-year food inflation variable is missing 635 observations, and most notably, the food insecurity variable has 4,295 missing values. This suggests that the coverage of the food insecurity data is more limited than the other datasets. This could be a reflection of the differences in country coverage over the years, as well as missing observations within the original dataset. Further investigation is needed to determine how usable this merged dataset is for analysis. 
<h2>Timeliness</h2>
Because of the differing time scales used by each individual dataset, the merged one can only consider up to the common time frame with all three. As a result, the dataset can only go up to the end of the calendar year 2025.
